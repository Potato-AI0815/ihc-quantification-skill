## I. Known limitations

- OME-TIFF metadata workflow remains experimental.
- Packed native 12-bit TIFF is not formally validated.
- Puncta detection is `VALIDATED_FOR_SYNTHETIC_AGGREGATE_COUNTING`; no
  coordinate-level precision/recall/F1 detector validation.
- Colocalization does not establish molecular binding.
- HPA validation is ordinal concordance, not clinical validation.
- Weak external results remain unchanged and disclosed: BBBC007, BBBC016, and
  HPA are `PASS_WITH_WARNINGS`.
